Excluded Columns & Operational Boundaries

The following features were intentionally withheld from model features to protect fairness and intake timing integrity.

Sex & Age / AgeOfPolicyHolder

Protected demographic classes. Omitted to avoid proxy discrimination and comply with fair insurance lending/handling standards.

Fault & NumberOfSuppliments

Post-intake operational flags. Not available at claim intake time; including them would cause target leakage.

PolicyNumber & RepNumber

Arbitrary row counters and internal adjuster IDs that have zero causal relation to fraud risk.

Claim Year

Excludes annual macro drifts and policy changes from being memorized as static fraud markers.
