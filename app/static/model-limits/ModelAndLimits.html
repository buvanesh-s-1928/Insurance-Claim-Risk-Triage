How a claim is scored

  1. Each of the 22 categorical and numeric claim fields is encoded without identifiers.
  2. A regularized logistic-style classifier estimates the probability of past fraud among comparable claims.
  3. Contributions for each field are computed relative to the 6.0% overall baseline rate.
  4. Claims are sorted from highest probability to lowest, and your capacity slider sets where the High band ends.

Limits of this prototype

  • Trained on 1994 to 1996 claims; vehicle makes and policy structures have changed.
  • Labels capture only found fraud, not undetected fraud or mistaken referrals.
  • No claim dollar amounts are present in the dataset.
  • Unconfirmed dataset license; intended strictly for prototype evaluation.
  • Validated on one public dataset only (3,084 held-out test claims).

Decision support only. A high rank means look at this claim first. It does not show that fraud occurred, and this tool never rejects a claim.
