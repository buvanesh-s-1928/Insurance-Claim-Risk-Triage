Audited Exclusion Register

Features explicitly excluded from training to prevent bias, target leakage, or ungrounded discrimination.

Sex Protected demographic attribute

Excluded from all feature encoding to enforce gender parity under regulatory compliance.

Age / AgeOfPolicyHolder Protected demographic attribute

Excluded to avoid age discrimination and prevent young/elderly driver bias.

MaritalStatus Proxy for demographic status

Excluded to eliminate marital or domestic profile stereotyping.

PolicyNumber / RepNumber Row counters & internal IDs

Identifies claim records and internal adjusters, but contains no inherent claim risk signal.

NumberOfSuppliments Post-incident supplemental payments

Known only later during settlement; excluded to prevent post-hoc target leakage.

DriverRating Uniform distribution (zero correlation)

Historical fraud rates are mathematically identical across all driver rating tiers.

Decision support only. A high rank means inspect first; it does not constitute a finding of fraud.
