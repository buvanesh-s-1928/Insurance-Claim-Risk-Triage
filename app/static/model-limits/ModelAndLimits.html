This tool never rejects a claim or labels a claimant as fraudulent. Human review is mandatory.

Feature Governance & Anti-Leakage Register

Fields deliberately excluded from the gradient boosting model to prevent data leakage and unconstitutional proxy discrimination.

PolicyNumber Row counter; identifies the claim but says nothing about it.
RepNumber The adjuster who handled the claim; not a property of the claim.
Deductible May be decided during claim handling, so it may not exist at intake.
DriverRating Counts extra payments made after the claim; known only later.
Days:Policy-Claim Fraud rate is the same at every level; behaves like noise.
Sex Protected attribute; used only to check whether groups are flagged unevenly.
MaritalStatus Protected attribute; and 320 values are missing (stored as 0).
Age Age of the policyholder; same concern as age proxy.
AgeOfPolicyHolder Possible stand-in for protected attributes.
Year Claim year; fraud rate drifts over the three years, so model should not learn it.

Ethical Governance Notice: Review Priority, Not Proof of Fraud Human Review Mandatory

Algorithmic fraud probability scores and SHAP attribution metrics are decision-support triage recommendations. The system never denies or rejects claims automatically. An adverse determination requires complete independent investigation by a qualified human claims investigator.
